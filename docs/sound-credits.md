# Sound credits

cinetic's effects catalogue, [`sfx-catalogue.json`](../skills/cinetic/scripts/audio/sfx-catalogue.json), holds links, never audio: each film fetches the sounds it plays at film time, with a licence record beside each file, and nothing is committed. None of these licences requires a credit. They are credited here anyway, and every film's `CREDITS.md` names the sounds it played.

## Motionstash

Thanks to [Motionstash](https://x.com/motionstashco), who picked and shared a library of sound effects for motion design. 25 of their picks are in the catalogue. They are Pixabay sounds (the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): commercial use, no credit required, never passed on as a file by itself), so a film fetches each one from Motionstash's shared folder and converts it to WAV. Each was heard with earshot and mixed over real music beds before it joined. The people who made them:

A game's, a brand's or a device's own sound never enters the catalogue. The other files were heard and left out for the reason the catalogue's `rejected` list gives (masked by the music, a cheap read, a kind already full).

## Kenney

29 sounds from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl)'s [UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio), [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) packs, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Mixkit

82 sounds from [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/), under the [Mixkit Sound Effects Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree).

## The ears

Every sound was judged with [earshot](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot): a forced choice between its role and cheap or cartoon alternatives, then mixed at its role's level over real music beds.
