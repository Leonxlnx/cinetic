# The cinetic mark

A lowercase c drawn as a ring, its upper end breaking into two film frames, and a caret standing in
its opening.

- **The ring** is a zoetrope seen from above, the oldest machine for making pictures move: cine + kinetic.
- **The two frames** coming off its end are what it makes. They are red and teal.
- **The caret** is the coding agent. It is azure.

The mark was made with the skill itself, for the cinetic 1.0 announcement film (brand step: nine
directions on a sheet, a blind misread test at 64 px, a lockup sheet).

## Drawing

On a 100-unit box: the ring is centred at (42, 50), outer radius 40, inner radius 23, open 84 degrees
on the right. Clockwise from the lower lip: the body (164 degrees), then two frames of 38 degrees,
with gaps of 18 degrees. The caret is 14 x 88 units at x 84, with round ends.

| Colour | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ink | `#0D1012` |
| Paper | `#FCFDFE` |
| Azure (the caret) | `#0574C7` |
| Red (the first frame) | `#D8413C` |
| Teal (the second frame) | `#11938C` |

The name is set in Hanken Grotesk 600, tracked -0.04 em. In a lockup the mark is 1.1 times the
height of the name's ascenders and sits a third of its own width from the name.

## Use

Keep the caret azure, the frames red and teal, and the body of the c in ink on light grounds and
paper on dark ones. Leave clear space of at least the caret's width around the mark. Do not rotate
the ring or redraw the caret as a square cursor.
