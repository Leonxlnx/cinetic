Concept: one task, start to finish, because the brief is literally one flow (one command deploys a model, and the
endpoint it returns carries the load and idles at zero); rejected: the number (the load alone cannot show the thirty
chores the command replaces), before and after (it would spend half the film on the chores). The worked examples all
show this concept: read them for moves, never for the concept.

# Forge: beats

Argument: running a model takes thirty things; Forge makes it one command, and the endpoint it gives you carries any
load and idles at zero. 16.1 s (484 f), 30 fps, on the bed's 122 BPM grid (beat 0 = frame 8, the main hit: the block
lands and the bed enters; `startFrom` 28.1).

World: a clean, bright block world. One light warm-grey field (#EEEDEA) as the ground, orderly isometric grids of
identical matte blocks (`Box bevel material="matte"`, lit from above, soft contact shadows, one light from the top
left), statements flat and centred on clean ground in ink, one colour a line; sun yellow (#FFC531) is the one accent and
means "live" (the lit model, the caret, the endpoint, the mark). Geist is the one voice; mono only inside the terminal.

Grounds: the grey field (the world), white paper (inside the model's top face: the terminal), sun (inside its lit face:
the load; the one ground cut in the accent). Ground cut 1 is the dive into the top face (grey to white), ground cut 2
the dive into the lit face (to sun), and the tiles take the face from sun back to white.

## Devices

- signature: the block (the model): it drops in (the hook), its top face is the terminal (the dive in and the snap back
  out), its lit face carries the load (the second dive: the proof), it unfolds into the F of the wordmark (the lockup)
- opening: drop-in, the model's block falls into frame and lands with weight on the bed's first beat
- text arrivals: an append that re-centres (`Build`), in ink; the wordmark's letters cut on one by one
- type treatment: statements alone and flat on clean ground; one line set on the model's white top face before the dive;
  the load set on the block's lit face
- seams: 2-3 the line lifts away as the camera pulls back over the grid | 3-4 the grid sinks outside in under the swing to
  the top face | 4-5 dive into the top face (signature; ground cut 1, paper) | 6-7 the large command drops into the
  log's first line | 7-8 snap back out of the face as the block rises and lights | 8-9 the load spills into the first
  replica | 9-10 dive into the lit face (signature; ground cut 2, sun) | 10-11 tiles: the face's cells switch off one by
  one as the count steps to zero (sun to white) | 11-12 the block splits into eight cubes and unfolds into the F
- spectacle: cascade, 24 replicas rising one after another in an orderly 5 x 5 grid behind the model, nearest first, the
  gaps compressing (5, 4, 3, then 2 f), the camera pulling back ahead of them to the widest frame, "25 replicas" counting
  flat under the grid, 2.2 s
- output-big: the load it carries, "12,480" requests a second at about 330 px on the block's lit face, then the steps to 0
- integrations: none (the film hinges on the command and the load, not on partners)
- lockup: a mark built from the film's object: the idle block splits into its eight cubes, which unfold into an F, each
  lighting sun as it lands; the F is the first letter of the wordmark, "orge" cut on beside it, forge.dev under it
- budget: no flood, no relay, no tour; one grid of chores, one grid of replicas; one count (the load, up and back to
  zero); one spectacle
