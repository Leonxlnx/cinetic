# Forge: beats

Argument: running a model takes thirty things; Forge makes it one command, and the endpoint it gives you works
everywhere, at any load. Device: the block. One block is the model; its top face is the terminal; its lit face
carries the load; it splits into the mark. 18.3 s, 30 fps, on the bed's 122 BPM grid (beat 0 = frame 8).

Grounds: ink (the world), paper (the terminal, entered through the block's top face), sun (the live endpoint floods
the frame; the pull-back reveals the sun was the block's lit face). Every cut is carried by an object.
